Games on Fundamental Concepts for Grades 1, 2 & 3

Grade - 1

Lesson 1 - World of Computers

Activity Name: World of Computers

Objective - Identifying types of computers (desktop, laptop, tablet, smartphone) and understanding their size, portability, and uses.


Grade - 1

Lesson 2 - Parts of Computer

Activity Name: Little Tech Explorer

Objective - Identifying parts of a computer and understanding how to connect them and turn the computer on.


Grade - 1

Lesson 3 - Click with Mouse

Activity Name: Pop the Balloons - Mouse Click

Objective - Practicing mouse control by clicking on objects accurately and improving hand-eye coordination.


Grade - 1

Lesson 4 - Type with Keyboard

Activity Name: Pop The Balloons - Type With Keyboard

Objective - Identifying keyboard keys and practicing typing letters, numbers, and simple words using correct keys.


Grade - 1

Lesson 5 - Explore Internet

Activity Name: Kiddle Town Explorer

Objective - Understanding terms like internet, browser, search engine, search term, search bar and website.

Lesson 6 - Online Safety

Activity Name: Click Smart

Objective - Understanding basic online safety rules and making safe decisions while using the internet.bri

Lesson 8 - Drawing With Shapes

Activity Name: Tangrams

Objective - Using basic shapes and colours in digital tools to create simple drawings.

Lesson 9 - Logic Building

Activity Name: Little Explorer

Objective - Sequencing and choosing the correct order of moves (up, down, left, right), figuring out the path to reach the goal while minimizing errors

Lesson 10 - Introduction to Block Coding

Activity Name: Robot Maze

Objective - Sequencing and choosing the correct order of moves (up, down, left, right), figuring out the path to reach the goal while minimizing errors


Grade - 2

Lesson 1 - Computer Peripherals

Activity Name: Peripherals Play

Objective - Identifying computer peripherals and understanding their functions through interactive questions.


Grade - 2

Lesson 2 - Right Click

Activity Name: Letters Express

Objective - Practicing right-click by selecting correct objects and actions.


Grade - 2

Lesson 3 - My Desktop

Activity Name: Icon Search

Objective - Identifying and selecting desktop icons.


Grade - 2

Lesson 5 - Learning with Kiddle

Activity Name: Kiddle Search Quest

Objective - Get familiar with the Kiddle browser, how to search for a search term, select and save an image.


Grade - 2

Lesson 6 - Passwords

Activity Name: Keep It Secret

Objective - Identifying strong passwords and keeping them secret.


Grade - 2

Lesson 9 - Introduction to Animation

Activity Name: Animation Challenge

Objective - Learn that playing frames one after another creates the illusion of movement, and build an animation by sequencing frames.


Grade - 2

Lesson 12 - Exploring Audio and Video

Activity Name: Media Player Mission

Objective - Identify audio, video, image and text file formats, choose the right app to open them, and use media player controls to play, pause, skip, shuffle and repeat songs.

Grade - 3

Lesson 1 - Explore Computers

Activity Name: Inside the Machine

Objective - Recognising everyday devices that are computers, identifying the main parts of a computer and what each part does, and sorting devices into input, output and networking.


Grade - 3

Lesson 2 - Troubleshooting

Activity Name: Helpdesk Hero

Objective - Spotting clues to diagnose common computer problems and fixing them safely.

Grade - 3

Lesson 3 - Computer Memory

Activity Name: Memory Movers

Objective - Understanding the difference between RAM and storage, ordering memory units from byte to terabyte, and sorting internal and portable storage devices.

Grade - 3

Lesson 4 - Browsers

Activity Name: Internet Postman

Objective - Building website addresses, using a browser to visit websites, following links to find information and practising safe browsing habits.

Grade - 3

Lesson 5 - Digital Citizenship

Activity Name: Netizen Badge Quest

Objective - Making safe and responsible choices online by solving real-life online scenarios and earning a badge for each digital citizenship rule.

Grade - 3

Lesson 6 - Creative Art

Activity Name: Art Studio

Objective - Exploring Tux Paint tools like Fill, Paint, Magic, Stamp and Shapes, and choosing the right one to create each part of a picture.
